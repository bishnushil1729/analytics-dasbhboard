🎛️ How to Use Interactive Features

  • Granularity Buttons: Click "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly", or "Yearly" to change time aggregation
  • Date Range Selector: Use buttons (1w, 1m, 3m, 6m, All) to zoom to specific periods
  • Range Slider: Drag the slider at bottom of each chart to select custom date ranges
  • Hover: Mouse over any point to see exact values
  • Zoom: Click and drag on chart to zoom into specific area
  • Pan: After zooming, drag to move left/right
  • Reset: Double-click anywhere on chart to reset view
